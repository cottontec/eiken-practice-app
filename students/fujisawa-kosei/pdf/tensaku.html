問題カードD　Online Music

音読

本文聞こえた読み直し方
and in this way they can...and in the way ...（と聞こえる）this を the と読んだか、th の音が弱い可能性。this way はひとかたまり。
and in this way / they canway の後で約1.8秒止まる意味の切れ目ではないので止まらない。
become more popularmore の後で約0.9秒止まるmore popular で1語のつもりで読む。

語の抜けはなく、全体は安定しています。止まる位置を意味の切れ目（カンマ・ピリオド）だけにすると、さらに聞きやすくなります。

質問への応答

No.1文法
According to the passage, how can people listen to songs outside even without an Internet connection?
×By people downloading their favorite music to their smartphones.
○By downloading their favorite music to their smartphones.
・By ＋ -ing で答えるときは主語（people）を入れない。内容は正解。
No.2文法
Tell me as much as you can about what the people in Picture A are doing.
×A boy is go up the stairs. A man is fixing a chair. A girl is feeding a dog. A woman is cooking. A woman is painting.
○A boy is going up the stairs. A man is fixing a chair. A girl is feeding a dog. A woman is cooking. A woman is painting the wall.
・is go → is going。「is ＋ ing形」を毎回そろえる。painting は何を塗っているか（the wall）まで言う。5人全員に触れられたのは良い点。1人ごとに2〜3秒の沈黙があるので、言い終えたらすぐ次の人へ。
No.3今回いちばん大きい失点
Look at the woman and the man in Picture B. Please describe the situation.
×A man is looking for a ticket.
○The woman wants to see his ticket, but he can't find it.
・女性（車掌）に触れていないので、状況説明として半分です。吹き出し（切符を見せてほしい）と、男性が困っている理由を but でつなぐ。
No.4内容
Do you think listening to English songs is a good way for people to learn English?
×Yes. People can be interested in English.
○Yes. Listening to English songs makes people more interested in English. Also, they can learn new words while enjoying music.
・理由が1文で短い。English の直前で約5秒止まった。理由は2文、2つ目は Also, で足す型に固定する。

質問への応答

No.1今回いちばん大きい失点
According to the passage, why are some older people less likely to get lonely?【推定】
×Because some older people can see their children's faces through video calls.
○Because they can see their children's faces through video calls.
・本文の some older people は they に置き換える。内容は正解。準2級No.1で毎回出る型。
No.2語彙
Tell me as much as you can about what the people in Picture A are doing.【推定】
×A boy is climbing a tree. A woman is planting. A man is running to catch cats. Two (girls are) playing basketball. A man is throwing out.
○A boy is climbing a tree. A woman is planting flowers. A man is chasing a cat. Two girls are playing basketball. A man is throwing away some trash.
・目的語（flowers／trash）まで言い切る。猫は1匹なので a cat。「追いかける」は chase で言える。
No.3文法
Look at the man and the woman in Picture B. Please describe the situation.【推定】
×A man will carry a box because a woman cannot carry a box.
○She can't lift the box, so he is thinking of helping her carry it.
・男性はまだ考えている段階（吹き出し）なので will ではなく is thinking of ～ing。2回目からは the box／it。

×Yes. It is very convenient. (For th... Oh, sorry.) It saves a lot of time.
○Yes. It is very convenient. Also, it saves a lot of time.
